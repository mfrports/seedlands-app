Privacy

Nothing collected.

Seedlands does not collect any data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and it sends nothing about you to anyone: not to us, and not to third parties.

Effective 11 November 2026

What stays on your device

Your seeds, the worlds you have opened, where you last looked and your settings are stored on your iPhone or iPad. Everything the map shows is worked out on your device from the seed.

What goes into your iCloud

A seed remembers where you have walked inside buried structures in live mode. So that this is not lost with the app or with a device, Seedlands also keeps it in your own iCloud (iCloud's key-value storage): the seed, the edition, and the places walked. It is your iCloud: it is not sent to us, and we cannot read it. With iCloud switched off it stays on the device only. Start over in the seed sheet wipes it, in iCloud as well.

Network

The game's building pieces. If you confirm that you own Minecraft and accept its licence (Seedlands asks first), the app downloads the game's own files from Mojang's servers (piston-meta.mojang.com and the download address it names), once per game version, so that structures look as they do in the game. This is an ordinary download: Mojang's servers see the request and your IP address, as with any download, and nothing else from Seedlands.

Live mode. If you turn it on, Seedlands announces itself on your local network so that your own game can find it, and receives from your game where you stand, which way you look, which dimension you are in, your player name, the game's version, the name of the world or server and, in single-player, the world's seed and how often a shrieker has warned the Warden of you. This stays on your network and on your device. If you connect to a server's console instead, Seedlands talks to the address you enter; the password you type is kept in the Keychain on your device.

Seedlands makes no other connections.

The mod

Seedlands Link, the companion mod for Minecraft: Java Edition, runs on your computer only. It looks for Seedlands on your local network and tells it what is listed under live mode above, and nothing else. It contacts no server on the internet.

Permissions

iOS asks for the Local Network permission the first time you open live mode. Seedlands uses it only to find your game on your network. The map works without it.

This website

These pages set no cookies, load nothing from other sites and count no visitors. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub sees the request and your IP address when you open a page or download the mod.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports. They contain no seeds and nothing that identifies you.

Children

Seedlands is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone.
