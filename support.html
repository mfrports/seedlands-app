Help with Seedlands

Something not working, or a map that doesn't match your world? Write to info@mfrports.com, or open an issue on GitHub (a free account is needed). Every message is read. Please say which edition and version you play, and the seed if you can share it.

Seedlands is made by Erik Muffler, who is responsible for it and answers at that address.

Your world

Where do I find my seed?

Java Edition: type /seed in the game's chat. Bedrock Edition: open the world's settings; the seed is listed under Game. On a server, ask whoever runs it.

The map doesn't match my world

Check three things in the seed sheet (tap the seed at the bottom): the seed, the edition (Java or Bedrock), and the version the area was generated in. A world started in an older version keeps its old terrain where you had already been. Seedlands shows the game's default world type; Amplified, Large Biomes, flat, modded and data-pack worlds are different.

Which versions are supported?

Java Edition 1.18.2 up to 26.3, and Bedrock Edition 1.18 up to 26.60. The Overworld and the Nether are mapped; the End is not yet.

Why does Seedlands ask whether I own Minecraft?

To show structures exactly as the game builds them, Seedlands fetches the game's own building pieces from Mojang's servers, once per version. Those files are Mojang's, and the licence to download them comes with the game. So Seedlands asks first, and fetches them only if you confirm that you own Minecraft and accept its licence. If you say Not now, the map works all the same, with Seedlands' own models; any structure's card offers the choice again.

What does a world remember, and how do I start it over?

Where you have walked inside buried structures in live mode is kept with the seed, on your device and in your iCloud, so it is still there on your other devices and after a fresh install. To wipe it, open the seed sheet (tap the seed at the bottom) and tap Start over under the seed. Undo takes that back for as long as the sheet stays open.

Finding things

How do I find the nearest village, stronghold or ancient city?

Tap Places, choose what you are looking for, and tap Nearest. The card shows its coordinates, and the arrows step on to the next ones.

What are the blue shapes in the ground?

Buried structures, shown as an x-ray: strongholds, ancient cities, trial chambers, trail ruins and buried treasure. Come closer and you can look into their rooms.

How do I get coordinates?

Touch and hold anywhere on the map for X, Y and Z, and the matching spot in the other dimension. Tap the readout at the top left to type or paste coordinates and fly there.

How do I get to the Nether, and back?

Tap the key with the arrow at the top right. The map dives down to the spot a portal would lead to; the same key brings you back up. A tap during the dive hurries it along.

Live mode

In live mode the map follows you while you play: it turns with your view and moves as you move.

Seedlands Link · the mod

For Java Edition, 26.1 or newer

  1. Install Fabric Loader for your version of the game.
  2. Download Seedlands Link and put the file in your game's mods folder.
  3. Start the game. On your iPhone or iPad, on the same Wi‑Fi, open Seedlands and tap the walking figure at the top right. Allow the local network when iOS asks.
  4. When the key lights up, tap it. The map takes your game's version and seed, and follows you.
Download Seedlands LinkAll about the mod

Seedlands Link runs on your computer only and talks to Seedlands on your own network. It tells the app your player name and the game's version, the name of the world or server you are in, where you stand, which way you look, which dimension you are in and, in single-player, the world's seed and how often a shrieker has warned the Warden of you. Nothing leaves your network.

The key doesn't light up

Both devices must be on the same Wi‑Fi, and Seedlands needs the Local Network permission (Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network). Guest networks and some routers keep devices from seeing each other.

The map stopped following me

Any touch on the map takes it back, so you can look around. The key at the top right lights up with an arrow pointing to you: one tap, and the map follows you again.

How do I choose how close the map rides?

Open the Live panel (the key at the top right) and turn the dial. The map still comes closer while you stand and stands off as you go faster.

What happens inside a buried structure?

Walk into an ancient city, a stronghold, trial chambers or trail ruins while the map follows you, and the land above lifts away: the structure's plan becomes the map, and it paints in where you have been. This needs the game's own building pieces (see Why does Seedlands ask whether I own Minecraft?).

What is the Warden's count?

Once a shrieker has gone off for you, a note over the bar says how many warnings stand (the Warden comes at the fourth) and counts down to the next one being forgotten: ten minutes of game time without a new one. Only the world itself knows that count, so it shows in single-player with the mod, and on a server you connected through its console.
