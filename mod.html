Seedlands Link · the mod

Play, and the map rides along.

Seedlands Link is the free companion mod for Minecraft: Java Edition. With it your game finds Seedlands on your Wi‑Fi by itself and tells it where you are. Nothing to type: no addresses, no passwords, no account.

Set it up

  1. Fabric Loader. Install Fabric Loader for your version of the game, if you don't have it yet. Seedlands Link needs nothing else, not even Fabric API.
  2. The mod. Download Seedlands Link and put the file into your game's mods folder. Start the game with the Fabric profile.
  3. The app. On your iPhone or iPad, on the same Wi‑Fi, open Seedlands and tap the walking figure at the top right. Allow the local network when iOS asks.
  4. One tap. When the key lights up, tap it. The map takes your game's version and seed, and follows you.

Where is the mods folder? In the Minecraft Launcher, open Installations, point at your Fabric installation and click the folder. The mods folder is in there; make it if it is missing.

What you get

  • The map follows you like a navigation system: it turns with your view and moves as you move, comes closer while you stand and stands off the faster you go.
  • You, in 3D, exactly where the game has you, and as you get about: on foot, gliding, in a boat or a minecart, on a horse, a pig, a camel or a strider, swimming and diving. Under the ground or in another dimension you stand on the map as a figure of light.
  • Your worlds, by name. In single-player the map takes each world's seed and the name you gave it, and changes with you when you leave one world for another.
  • Inside a structure, its plan becomes the map, and paints in where you have already been.
  • The Warden's warnings count down over the bar once a shrieker has gone off (single-player).
  • The map is still yours. Any touch takes it; one tap sets it following you again.

What it tells, and to whom

Seedlands Link runs on your computer only and talks to Seedlands on your own network. It tells the app your player name and the game's version, the name of the world or server you are in, where you stand, which way you look, which dimension you are in, what you ride, your pose (such as swimming or gliding), whether you are in the water and, in single-player, the world's seed and how often a shrieker has warned the Warden of you. Nothing leaves your network.

The mod looks for Seedlands on the local network by itself, the way a printer is found. Nothing is followed and no world is changed in the app until you tap the key there. More in Privacy.

Good to know

Does it work on a server?

Yes. The mod runs in your own game, so the server needs nothing. The map follows you just the same. Only the world's seed, its name and the Warden's warnings are the server's to know: there you enter the seed in Seedlands yourself, and the count is not shown.

Does it change my game?

No. It adds no blocks, items or commands and changes nothing in your world. It only reads where you are.

Which versions?

Minecraft: Java Edition 26.1 or newer, with Fabric Loader 0.18 or newer. Seedlands itself maps Java Edition from 1.18.2 and Bedrock Edition from 1.18; live mode with the mod is for Java Edition.

Updating

Put the new file into the mods folder, remove the older seedlands-link file, and start the game again.

What is new in 1.2.0?

It tells Seedlands how you get about (what you ride, whether you swim, dive or glide), so your figure on the map does the same. And it keeps talking when you leave a world: versions up to 1.1.0 went silent then, and the map stood still until the game was started again. If live mode stops after you change worlds, this update is the cure.

The key doesn't light up

Both devices must be on the same Wi‑Fi, and Seedlands needs the Local Network permission (Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network). Guest networks and some routers keep devices from seeing each other. Still dark? Support can help.
